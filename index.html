// About FlaxPlaza

Make the useful thing.
Use it thoughtfully.

FlaxPlaza brings together links and references for a more organized web experience. External services have their own terms and privacy practices; review them before sharing personal information.

  • Use strong, unique passwords.
  • Keep sensitive accounts off disposable inboxes.
  • Minimize unnecessary personal information.
  • Use research tools lawfully and with consent.
